Simple Ways to Help

No single action solves climate change, but people can make choices that reduce energy use and greenhouse gas emissions. Start with a step that fits your situation, then build new habits over time (U.S. EPA, n.d.).

Five Everyday Actions

  1. Turn off lights and electronics when they are not in use.
  2. Choose efficient lighting and appliances when practical.
  3. Walk, bike, carpool, or use public transportation when possible.
  4. Reduce food waste and reuse items before replacing them.
  5. Share reliable climate information with friends and family.
