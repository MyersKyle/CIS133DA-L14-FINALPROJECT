About This Website

Policy & Accessibility

Our Site Purpose

Climate Action: Protect Our Planet shares educational information about climate change and practical actions people can consider. It is an informational student project and is not an official website of NASA, NOAA, or the EPA.

Accessibility Statement

We want visitors to be able to use this site regardless of device or ability. We plan to use a clear heading structure, readable contrast and type, keyboard-accessible navigation, labeled form controls, responsive layouts, and text descriptions for images. If a visitor finds content difficult to use, they can consult the linked original sources.

Privacy and Sources

The pledge form is a browser demonstration; it does not request names, email addresses, or other personal details and does not send information to a server. Facts and externally sourced graphics will include attribution, with full citations in the project documentation and source credits on the relevant webpages.
