Why Learn About Climate Change?

Climate describes long-term patterns in temperature and weather. Climate change is a lasting change in those patterns. Scientists measure changes in global temperatures, ice, and sea levels to understand what is happening. Human activities, especially releasing heat-trapping gases, are a major driver of recent warming (NASA, n.d.).
